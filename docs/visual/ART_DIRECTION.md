# STILLFORM direction

Three words: considered, tactile, unhurried. A warm paper ground and near-black type frame real architectural texture. The site uses a monumental grotesk wordmark above a wide stair photograph, quiet serif accents and fine grid rules. There are no rounded product cards, lime accents, financial UI or geometric world scenes.

Hero: the spiral stair photograph fills a cinematic horizontal frame; muted warm tones, a subtle local dark scrim under white copy, and a small numbered caption. Portrait uses a tall centre crop rather than shrinking the desktop view. The title stays above the photograph in a carefully fitted width.

Project sequence: large left-hand residential image, narrower offset right-hand stair study, wide cultural image, narrow material facade. The asymmetric grid is intentional. Filters reduce to a regular pair/single composition and use Flip to retain spatial continuity. Captions separate study names from categories and use straightforward 'View study' actions.

Process: dark ground and sticky large numerals; a simple original line drawing relates to the section's concern. Final enquiry returns to warm paper. Footer title resolves the masthead scale.
